## Datathon FME 2026

6th edition, **14–15 November 2026**, School of Mathematics and Statistics (FME-UPC), C/ Pau Gargallo 14,
Barcelona. Organised by the Associació d'Estudiants de Dades with the FME. Three challenges; ours is the
Tinybird RawTree one. Questions about the challenge: the RawTree stand.

## What the calendar implies

- The system has to answer **all night** between Saturday and Sunday without anyone watching it, and it
  has to be the best version at Sunday 10:00. Plan a code freeze well before that.
- The presentation is at 14:00 on Sunday: the story (method, offline validation, learning from the stream,
  use of RawTree) and the demo need to be ready by then, and the repo must be readable for the jury.
