 What you build
 datathon.rawtree.dev
 A system that runs
on its own, all weekend.
 No files to upload and nobody pressing buttons:
your system is your submission.
 01
 Read requests.
 Each one describes a shopper
and what they just did in this session: device, region, page and the products they viewed.
 02
 Answer in 3 seconds.
 Write back an ordered list
of 10 product IDs. Late or invalid
answers are replaced by the best sellers.
 03
 Learn as you go.
 Private feedback on every answer
(clicks, purchases, revenue) and a shared stream of everything
happening in the store.
 RawTree
--- slide ---
 How it works
 datathon.rawtree.dev
 One loop,
three times a second.
 01
 A shopper arrives.
 A new record lands in shop.requests, about 3 per second.
 02
 Your system reads it.
 Poll the table, compute features, rank the catalogue.
 03
 You answer.
 Insert 10 product IDs into team_NN.submissions
within 3s.
 04
 The shopper reacts.
 A hidden model simulates the decision
to click and buy, and writes team_NN.feedback.
 Read your feedback and the live shop.events, improve, redeploy.
Every team gets the same shoppers at the same time.

 The data
 Every column is Dynamic · cast join keys: product_id::String
 Everything lives in RawTree.
 One API key per team: read the shared shop database,
read and write your own team_NN. JSON in, SQL out, no schema.
 shop.catalog
 ~5,000 products: category, subcategory, brand, price, a short description, active flag.
 5K recordsshared
 shop.history_sessions
 60 days of carousel impressions from the old system: shown, clicked, bought, plus propensities.
 2M recordsshared
 shop.history_events
 60 days of organic activity outside the carousel: views, add-to-cart, purchases.
 12M recordsshared
 shop.requests
 One record per shopper arriving at the carousel. This is what you answer.
 3 / slive
 shop.events · catalog_updates
 Store activity in real time (spot the trends) and catalogue changes: new products, prices, stock-outs.
 ~50 / slive
 team_NN.feedback · scratch
 What happened with each of your answers, only visible to you. Plus a free table for your own features.
 privateyour team
