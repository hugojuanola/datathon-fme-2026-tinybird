 Real-time recommendation for shoppers who won't wait
 A shopper arrives every few seconds. You have 3 seconds to choose the 10 products they will see. A hidden model simulates each shopper's decision to click and buy.

 The challenge
 Sell more than the best sellers
 On the home page and on every product page there is a row of ten recommended products. Right now it just shows the best sellers of the category the shopper is looking at. Build something that sells more, tested live on shoppers arriving in real time.
 01 Read requests
 Every request describes a shopper and what they just did in this session: device, region, page and the products they viewed.
 02 Answer in 3 seconds
 Write back an ordered list of 10 product IDs. Late or invalid answers are replaced by the best sellers.
 03 Learn as you go
 Private feedback on every answer (clicks, purchases, revenue) and a shared stream of everything happening in the store.
 How it works
 Your system is your submission
 No files to upload: a loop that runs on its own, all weekend, against RawTree.
 STEP 1 A shopper arrives
 A new record lands in shop.requests , about 3 per second.
 STEP 2 Your system reads it
 Poll the table, compute features, rank the catalogue.
 STEP 3 You answer
 Insert 10 product IDs into team_NN.submissions in 3 s.
 STEP 4 The shopper reacts
 A hidden model simulates the decision to click and buy, and writes team_NN.feedback .
 Read your feedback and the live shop.events stream, improve, redeploy. Every team gets the same shoppers at the same time.

 Two yardsticks
 0 → 100
 Baseline (0) = the 10 all-time best sellers that are in stock (the starter kit). Oracle (100) = the 10 products the hidden model itself would pick. The leaderboard also shows the organisers' reference recommender as a benchmark. It can be beaten.
 A request is cold when the shopper is new ( is_new_user ) or their recent_views include a product added during the event.
 The jury part is a short presentation with a demo on Sunday afternoon. Tie-breaker: expected revenue per request in the final round.

 Clone the repo

 Run the baseline

 FAQ
 Questions teams ask
 Can we use AI coding agents and LLMs? Yes. Everything you present has to be yours to explain: anything a team cannot explain may be penalised.
 What happens if my system is down or slow? That shopper sees the best sellers (the fallback) and you lose reliability points. Your feedback tells you why: missing , late or invalid .
 Does the practice round count? No. Only Sunday 10:00–12:00 counts. Practice is for learning, and the public leaderboard shows the last hour.
 Can I keep changing my code during the final? Yes, your system keeps answering while you work. You will not see the ranking until 12:00, so have your best version running by 10:00.
 Do we need a server? No, a laptop works if it stays awake and online all night. Requests not answered get the fallback.
 "Illegal type Dynamic" in my SQL? RawTree is schemaless: every column is Dynamic . Cast join keys and a few aggregates, e.g. a.product_id::String = b.product_id::String .
 Why do queries on shop.requests fail before Saturday 10:00? The live tables ( shop.requests , shop.events , shop.catalog_updates ) are empty until the stream opens, so their columns do not exist yet and you get Unknown expression identifier . They fill at 10:00; the baseline just retries. Explore the history meanwhile.
 Does luck affect the ranking? No. Whether a shopper buys is random, so the score uses each shopper's probability of buying the products you showed, from the simulator's hidden model, not the coin flip. Your feedback shows the realised clicks and purchases; on average they agree. A shopper who already bought a product tends not to want it again, so real purchases still feed later scores a little.
 Is the data real? No. Shoppers, sessions and outcomes come from a simulator with a secret seed. Finding a public dataset gives you nothing you do not already have.
 Can we share keys or feedback with other teams? No. Feedback is private to each team, and recommendations must be produced by your code.
